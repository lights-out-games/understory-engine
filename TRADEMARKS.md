# Name and logo

"Understory" is a working name. It has not been searched or registered, and it may change before a first public release.

Once a name is settled, this policy applies. The code licences (MIT OR Apache-2.0) do not grant rights to the project's name or logo.

**You may, without asking:**

- Say that your product is "built with" or "powered by" the engine.
- Use the name to refer to the unmodified project, in articles, talks, package descriptions and documentation.

**You may not, without permission:**

- Use the name or logo as the name of a fork, a modified build or a product.
- Suggest that the project endorses or is affiliated with your product.

Forks are welcome and must use a different name. This document is a draft and is not legal advice.
